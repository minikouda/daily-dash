Daily Dash

A personal email digest that runs on my own computer.

Daily Dash reads my own Gmail accounts (read-only) every morning and turns them into a short briefing, a to-do list of deadlines and follow-ups, and a package tracker. Tasks sync to Apple Reminders.

It is a personal tool, not a public service: it runs locally on the developer's Mac and only accesses accounts the developer owns.
